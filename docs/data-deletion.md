# Data Deletion Request (Flipzy)

Flipzy respects your privacy. If you want to request deletion of your personal data associated with Flipzy, follow the steps below.

## What data can be deleted
Depending on your usage, we may delete:
- Uploaded photos (if stored on our servers)
- Generated images/videos (if stored on our servers)
- App identifiers used for service operation (e.g., anonymous user ID or device-linked identifiers)
- Purchase-related metadata needed for support (if applicable)

Note: If some content is stored only on your device (e.g., saved to your Photos/Library), you can delete it directly from your device.

## How to request deletion
Send an email to:

**Email:** mehmetoztin@gmail.com  
**Subject:** Flipzy Data Deletion Request

In your email, include:
- The email address you want us to use for communication
- A short message requesting deletion
- (Optional) Any relevant details that help us locate your data (e.g., approximate date of use)

## Verification
To protect users, we may ask you to verify ownership of the account/data. This may include confirming details available in the app or providing proof of purchase if relevant.

## Processing time
We typically complete deletion requests within **7–30 days**, depending on the scope of the request and verification.

## Contact
If you have any questions, contact us at mehmetoztin@gmail.com
